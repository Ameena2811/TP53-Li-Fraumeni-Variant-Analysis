# In-Silico Analysis of TP53 Variants in Li-Fraumeni Syndrome

## Overview & Methods
A total of 20 *TP53* variants classified as Uncertain Significance in ClinVar were selected. Variant information (HGVS nomenclature) was first retrieved from Ensembl. ClinVar data including classification, review status, associated conditions, and last evaluated dates were obtained from the NCBI ClinVar database. In-silico pathogenicity predictions, particularly AlphaMissense class and score, were taken from VarSome. Additional predictors (CADD, REVEL, SIFT, PolyPhen) and population allele frequencies (gnomAD) were also recorded.

## Results & Discussion
All 20 selected variants are currently classified as Uncertain Significance in ClinVar (mostly with 1- or 2-star review status). However, AlphaMissense consistently predicted them as Pathogenic Strong with very high scores (mostly >0.99). CADD, REVEL, SIFT, and PolyPhen also indicated damaging effects. Almost all variants showed extremely low or absent allele frequencies in gnomAD, supporting their rarity.

These findings suggest that the selected variants are strong candidates for potential reclassification to Likely Pathogenic.
